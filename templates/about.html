A story of transformation and resilience

JEEVAN SETU began as a college initiative with a vision to create meaningful impact in the lives of people. Our aim is to bridge critical gaps in health, accessibility, and awareness through sustainable and scalable solutions.

Our Mission

To eliminate the barriers in patient-hospital communication and offer a smart, easy-to-use platform that brings healthcare services within everyone’s reach — through clean technology and thoughtful design.

Our Vision

To create a unified healthcare experience where patients no longer struggle with delays, confusion, or lack of access — but instead feel empowered, informed, and supported every step of the way.

Our Strategy

  • Identify common gaps in healthcare access and communication

  • Build user-centric tools like dashboards, chat assistants, and pharmacy modules

  • Focus on performance, ease of use, and adaptability

  • Deliver a platform that works efficiently with minimal tech resources

What We’re Solving

  • Lack of real-time communication between patients and hospitals

  • Confusion during hospital visits due to unclear processes

  • Delays in accessing prescriptions and healthcare information

  • Absence of self-help support for frequently asked queries

    • Jeevan Setu is the first platform to eliminate communication gaps between patients and healthcare providers.
